- `powerful`, `robust`, `seamless`, `empowers`, `unlock`, `optimal`, `comprehensive`, `future-proof`, `scalable`: inflated words replacing concrete mechanics.
- `At its core` and `The key takeaway`: empty labels announcing the next sentence.
- Perfect first/second/final structure: symmetrical organization not earned by the material.
- `potential trade-offs`: mentions trade-offs without naming one.
- `I’m confident`: confidence without evidence.
- `Exciting times ahead!`: grand closing unrelated to an action.
